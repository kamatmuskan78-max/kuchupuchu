Ek Khat 💌

Ek Khat Tumhare Naam... 💌

Meri Jaan,

Shayad main roz tumhe ye nahi bata paati ki tum mere liye kya maine rakhte ho. Lekin sach yehi hai ki mere har khayal mein, har dua mein sirf tumhara hi naam hota hai.

Tumne mere andheron mein ek nayi roshni la di hai. Mere tute hue hisson ko apne pyaar se jod diya hai. Ek ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai tumhari awaaz sunkar, jaise saari thakan utar gayi ho.

Jab bhi main apna aage ka lamba safar dekhti hoon, toh mujhe us raste par sirf tumhara haath apne haath mein chahiye. Tum meri taqat ho, meri khushi ho, aur meri sabse khubsurat aadat ho.

Thank you for being my peace, my home, and my love.

Hamesha, sirf tumhari. ❤️
