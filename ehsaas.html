Ehsaas 💙

Tumse Baat Karna

Pura din kaisa bhi gaya ho, par aakhir mein jab tumse baat hoti hai, lagta hai jaise duniya ki saari pareshaniyan khatam ho gayi hain. Tum mera safe space ho.

Tumhari Smile

Tumhari ek sachi muskurahat mein itni taqat hai ki wo mere sabse bure din ki bhi saari thakan aur udaasi pal bhar mein chura leti hai.

Tumhari Care

Jis tarah tum choti-choti cheezon ka dhyan rakhte ho, mujhe lagta hai ki main kitni lucky hoon. Meri zindagi aasan bana di tumne.

Tumhari Presence

Baat karne ke liye shabdon ki zarurat hi nahi. Bas tumhara aas-paas hona hi is duniya ke shor ko shant karne ke liye kaafi hota hai.

Tumhari Yaadein

Din bhar mein aane wale saikdon khayalon ke beech, tumhari yaad ek thandi hawa ke jhonke jaisi hai jo chehre par muskaan laati hai.

Tum Mere Liye Kya Ho

Tum meri manzil bhi ho, aur wahan tak ka safar bhi. Tumhe dekh kar kal ki khubsurat tasveer nazar aati hai. Tum meri sabse badi umeed ho.
