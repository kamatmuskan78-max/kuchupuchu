Pehli Mulaqat

Wo din jab humari raahein pehli baar mili, achanak ek khamosh sa connection jud gaya tha. Jaise dono ko kisi ka intezaar tha, aur us ek pal mein wo talaash khatam ho gayi.

❤️

Raat Bhar Baatein

Waqt ka pata hi nahi chala. Tumhe jaanna, tumhari choti-choti baatein sunna, ek aadat si ban gayi. Har subah tumhare message ka intezaar hone laga, aur har raat tumhari awaaz ke bina adhoori lagne lagi.

❤️

Kuch Pyaare Lamhe

Wo choti-choti khushiyan jo humne saath baanti. Chhoti baaton par hasna, aur bina kahe ek dusre ko samajhna. Zindagi achanak se bahut haseen lagne lagi.

❤️

Ehsaas-e-Ishq

Wo pal jab achanak samajh aaya, ki tumhare bina ab zindagi adhoori si lagegi. Wo khamosh pal jab dil ne maan liya ki yahi meri manzil hai.
